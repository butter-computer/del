# Del 0.1.23

The left sidebar is easier to grab and can collapse and reopen within one continuous drag.

- Hide the entire Space indicator row when only one Space exists.
- Expand the invisible left-sidebar resize target from 6 to 16 logical points, with the resize cursor across the full target.
- Collapse after dragging 80 logical points past the minimum width. Keep holding the mouse and drag back to reopen, then continue resizing.
- Separate the close and reopen thresholds by 32 points to prevent flickering near the boundary.
- Reverse sidebar animations from their current position and respect Reduce Motion. Command-B and the title-bar toggle retain the last valid width.

This installer also includes the source updates since the previous public installer, 0.1.20: sidebar tools in the title bar, persistent Spaces, Space-specific or global session tabs, and project moves between Spaces. See the [0.1.21 notes](https://github.com/butter-computer/del/blob/v0.1.23/docs/releases/0.1.21.md) and [0.1.22 notes](https://github.com/butter-computer/del/blob/v0.1.23/docs/releases/0.1.22.md) for details.

## Install

Download `Del-0.1.23-arm64.dmg`, open it, and drag Del.app to Applications. Requires an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 11 or later. Provider CLIs and their authenticated accounts are separate.

The public app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. The release includes a SHA-256 sidecar and `latest.json` for the in-app updater. Local development builds retain their separate signing identity.

See the [validation record](https://github.com/butter-computer/del/blob/v0.1.23/docs/releases/0.1.23-validation.md).
