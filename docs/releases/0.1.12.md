# Del 0.1.12

- More compact tabs throughout the app, with tighter edge fades and full visibility when selecting overflowed tabs or restoring them on launch.
- The left sidebar resizes from the grey gutter between the sidebar and chat, clear of tab and conversation controls.
- Smoother opening and closing across top-level, main-inner, and right-sidebar tabs. Rapid actions remain responsive, transfers settle immediately, and Reduced Motion skips transitions.
- Side chat replies completed out of view now show unread blue dots on the fork tab and its parent in the top bar and left sidebar. Unread state survives app restarts and clears independently for each fork.
- Working Side chats show spinners on their own tabs and parent session indicators. Sending in a Side chat keeps the parent session tab open.
- Includes the 0.1.10 pane refinements: more room for the Files tree, readable black terminals in light mode, and a short divider centered in the gutter beside the inner tab row.

Apple Silicon, macOS 11 or later. This release is **ad-hoc signed and not notarized**, matching the previous public release. It includes the DMG, SHA-256 sidecar, and `latest.json` updater manifest. Local development builds retain their separate signing identity.

See the [validation report](https://github.com/butter-computer/del/blob/v0.1.12/docs/releases/0.1.12-validation.md).
