# Del 0.1.22

Spaces organize projects, pinned chats, and session tabs into separate workspaces.

- Create and rename Spaces from the sidebar plus menu. The heading shows the Space name, and compact dots above Settings show the active Space.
- Swipe horizontally with two fingers to slide between Spaces. Lists follow finger travel and settle on release; short or cancelled swipes return smoothly. Fixed the swipe-release crash.
- Keep session tabs specific to each Space, or enable Global session tabs in Appearance. Changing this preference keeps Settings open.
- Move a project to another Space from its project menu. Existing destination projects merge their session tabs and pins without duplicates or interrupted agents.
- Delete Space appears in red only when another Space exists. Projects, pins, and tabs move into the first remaining Space; chats and files are preserved.
- Space collections, pins, ordering, drafts, and tab selections survive restarts. Background sessions retain their original ownership.
- Compact menus and borderless name dialogs reduce visual clutter. The Space menu plus rotates on open and returns on dismissal, respecting Reduce Motion.

This is a source update; the downloadable installer remains 0.1.20.
