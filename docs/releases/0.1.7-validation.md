source-slot collapse and plus-button placement, insertion gaps and reordering,

placement while moving the pointer vertically. The original conversation remains
fixed, and live tool entities retain their state during a transfer.

tests. Prior provider checks and their environment-dependent limitations are
recorded in [0.1.6 validation](0.1.6-validation.md); they are not claimed as fresh

remains, without failing current builds or strict Clippy.

The public DMG is ad-hoc signed and **not notarized**. The development bundle uses
a separate persistent local signing identity.
