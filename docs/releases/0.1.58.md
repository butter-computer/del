# Del 0.1.58

The Processes popup is narrower, down from 364 to 260 points at default zoom.
Hovering a heading or process highlights the full row. The highlight meets the
popup’s rounded corners without a gap above the first row or below the last.
Click anywhere on a heading to expand or collapse its section.

Download **Del-0.1.58-arm64.dmg** for Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 11 or later.
The public app is ad-hoc signed and unnotarized. The release includes a SHA-256
checksum and the `latest.json` manifest for the in-app updater.
