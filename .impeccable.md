## Design Context

### Users
Del is for people working with coding agents across Codex, Claude Code, and other harnesses, switching between conversations and terminals.

### Brand Personality
Compact, calm, responsive. Keep controls familiar and avoid unnecessary labels and visual clutter.

### Aesthetic Direction
Follow the user’s ChatGPT/Codex references: black dark-mode workspaces, near-black chrome, subtle raised surfaces, provider accent colors, compact rows, and quiet scroll fades. Use blue for actions, green for success, and amber for caution; preserve the separate light palette. Support light and dark themes.

### Design Principles
- Preserve room for conversation content and readable session titles.
- Use short, smooth transitions with reduced-motion alternatives.
- Present actionable failures in dismissible toasts, without shifting the composer.
- Keep attachment previews and follow-ups close to the input.
- Avoid decorative dropdown arrows and hover drag icons where the user requested none.
